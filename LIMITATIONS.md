# Limitations

- Difference-in-differences relies on parallel pre-treatment trends.
- Treatment assignment was not random.
- Matching improves comparability but cannot eliminate unobserved confounding.
- The available analysis uses a limited number of pre- and post-period observations.
- Store-level spillovers may contaminate control locations.
- Province indicators do not capture every local market difference.
- The break-even threshold depends on cost assumptions that should be independently validated.
- Statistical non-significance does not prove that the true effect is exactly zero.
