# Results

## Headline result

Raw pilot-store growth averaged 1,318 units per store, which appears to exceed the 1,100-unit threshold. After subtracting contemporaneous growth in the control group, the estimated effect fell to 840 units without matching and 790 units with matched controls.

The adjusted estimates were below the break-even threshold and did not reach statistical significance in the available sample.

## Regional pattern

Ontario and British Columbia showed the strongest adjusted effects. Alberta and Manitoba were substantially below the threshold. Quebec produced positive lift but remained below the required level after control adjustment.

## Decision implication

The evidence does not support immediate expansion across the full network. A smaller continuation in the strongest regions can preserve learning while limiting financial exposure, provided that the next phase uses stronger pre-registration, matched controls, and additional post-treatment observations.

## Important distinction

The gross profit calculation based on raw pilot-store lift should not be treated as causal profit. It includes background market growth that would likely have occurred without the program.
